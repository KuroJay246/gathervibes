# Registration Payment Review and Action Standard

## Organizer workflow

1. Use Registration Payments to identify guest payment follow-up first.
2. Use internal review only for contradictions, missing evidence, or cleanup.
3. Open the payment detail panel before deciding on the next action.
4. Use `Create Follow-Up Task` when the work should move into the event task workflow.
5. Use `Open Registration` when the underlying registration record itself needs correction.

## Guardrails

- Do not treat internal cleanup as proof that a guest still owes money.
- Do not move registration money into Operations unless it is intentionally a separate event-level ledger entry.
- Do not use Tasks to grant access or to store private payment proof.
- Do not recalculate balances outside the existing finance utilities.
