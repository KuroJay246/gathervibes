# Operations Presentation and Commitment Standard

## Organizer workflow

1. Use Ledger for recorded event-level money.
2. Use Commitments for unpaid or partially paid obligations.
3. Use Partners and Suppliers to review who is connected to those obligations.
4. Use In-Kind Support for non-cash contributions only.
5. Use task-prefill links when a follow-up should become explicit event work.

## Guardrails

- Registration payments are not Operations income.
- Requested sponsorship is not received money.
- Outstanding commitments are not paid expenses.
- In-kind support is not cash.
- Current Ledger Difference is not final profit.
